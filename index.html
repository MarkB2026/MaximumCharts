Why IBKR?

We build our broker-connected trading tools around Interactive Brokers because its API, broad market access, and sophisticated order capabilities make it an exceptional foundation for serious charting and trading automation.

Rather than dilute our software across dozens of integrations, we focus on doing one platform exceptionally well. Want to use our broker-connected charting tools? Start with Interactive Brokers.

What runs where?

Maximum Charts Basic: Windows desktop charting with IBKR-connected market data, essential indicators and drawing tools.

Maximum Charts Premium: Windows desktop charting with IBKR connectivity, advanced indicators, dynamic layouts and pattern recognition.
